# Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyse Superstore sales performance, product profitability, and customer purchasing behaviour. The goal is to turn business data into useful insights that can support better business decisions.

## Dashboard 1: Sales Overview

This dashboard analyses sales trends over time, compares sales with profit, and examines sales performance by region and country. It helps assess overall business performance, identify strong markets, and understand whether increased sales are translating into higher profits.

![Sales Overview](sales-overview)

## Dashboard 2: Product Performance

This dashboard identifies products and subcategories that generate profit and those that generate losses. It helps distinguish products that sell well from products that are genuinely profitable and highlights areas where pricing, discounting, or cost management may need further investigation.

![Product Performance](product-performance)

## Dashboard 3: Customer Insights

This dashboard examines the top customers and compares one-time customers with returning customers. It helps show which customers contribute most to business performance and provides insight into repeat purchasing and customer retention opportunities.

![Customer Insights](customer-insights)
